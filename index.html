FAIR PLAY POLICY

Tournament Rules

1. Respect every player

No abusive language, harassment, or deliberate disruption. Keep communication respectful.

2. Play honestly

Cheats, exploits, modified clients, and fake results are not allowed. Verified violations may lead to disqualification.

3. Match timing

Players should be ready at the announced start time. Contact the organizer if an opponent does not arrive.

4. Submit proof

Submit the final score and a clear screenshot through Results. Do not edit or fabricate evidence.

5. Entry fee and prizes

Check each tournament's published entry fee, prize pool, game version, format, and rules before joining. This website's wallet and shop screens are currently visual demos.

6. Organizer decision

Raise disputes with the organizer and provide evidence. Decisions should be based on the published rules and available proof.
